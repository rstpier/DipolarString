# Status of archived packages

These ZIP files are immutable historical evidence, not current verification inputs.

## V2.7 package

Useful retained infrastructure: 40-test manifest, symbolic identities, Johns uniqueness
scan, breather calculation, licences, environment record and run logs.

Superseded physical outputs include:

- hydrostatic source `u` instead of the A8 source `u-u0`;
- critical mass `1.616e6 Msun` and compactness `1.217`;
- Bell-corridor impedance and separation bounds;
- physical interpretation of the scalar photon sphere and 2PN deviations;
- wording that treats the Heaviside profile as a solution beyond its linearized scope.

## V2.8 Johns sessions

- Session 1 independently reconstructs the Johns node and low-q bands.
- Sessions 2–3 study defect localization and dispersion.
- Session 4 establishes that the electron-frequency target lies in the bulk continuum for
  the minimal straight defect.
- Session 5 finds incompatible `S` and `E` target tensions for a single line defect.
- Session 6 finds closure sectors near `+40 keV` and `-126 keV`, including an unresolved
  negative-energy interpretation; no `511 keV` invariant emerges.

The negative results are retained because they constrain future versions of the model.
